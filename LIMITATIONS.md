# What this case cannot establish

**Synthetic case; no real certification client or professional is represented.** The original delivery brief supports that setting. It does not supply empirical evidence about any actual credentialing program. [E01]

## Substantive and design limits

The scoring audit verifies consistency with the delivered records, key and rule. It does not establish external authenticity, correct item content or the authority and substantive basis of the standard. No independently validated competence criterion was supplied, so classification accuracy is not identifiable. No historical certification error, defective key or replacement standard follows from the current evidence. [E10] [E08]

Item wording, alternatives' rationales, detailed blueprint targets and response-process evidence are absent. ANC_14's negative functioning is not proof of a wrong key; ANC_18's conditional regional difference is not demonstrated bias; B_11's weak discrimination is not automatic invalidity. Fictional region labels are not inferred demographic or protected attributes. [E02] [E06] [E07]

First-attempt form groups differ, and assignment/equivalence has not been independently established. Observed pass-rate differences cannot be attributed solely to form difficulty. Common-item designation and close fitted curves do not establish invariance or operational interchangeability. Selected retakes are not independent external validation. [Q09] [Q10] [MP07] [MP09]

## Statistical limits

Complete-case selection, tetrachoric conditioning, weak secondary dimensions, normal-latent identification, signed response functions, ignorable omissions and local independence remain conditional assumptions. No family-wise Q3 exceedance is not proof of independence. Fixed-calibration residual reference simulation omits item-estimation uncertainty. DIF matching is estimated from the same response data and ordinary inference conditions on it. [MP03] [MP04] [MP05] [MP06]

The provisional observed-score correspondence A=42 / B=38.70 has a 95% bootstrap interval 38.15-39.13 under the primary common-reference model. **This is not an authorized conversion or recommended new passing cut.** Fixed anchor selection, content/design uncertainty, discrete-score conventions and reference-population assumptions limit its use. The interval is neither a competence interval nor complete uncertainty about certification policy. [Q12] [Q13] [Q14] [Q15] [MP08]

Global and near-boundary agreement describe hypothetical independent administrations at unchanged proficiency. They are not observed retests, true-error rates or probabilities that a real candidate deserves certification. Bootstrap parameter/link intervals exclude important structural and substantive uncertainty. Internal numerical agreement and same-data sensitivity are not independent empirical replication. [MP09] [E08]

## Reproduction and release limits

Tier 1 verifies data and retained evidence and recomputes a declared subset using unchanged core functions. It does not refit every model or regenerate all uncertainty draws. Tier 2 complete replay is unavailable as distributed: original stage orchestration, historical inventories, processed releases and complete calibration/link draw caches are not packaged. The [replay plan](docs/FULL_REPLAY_PLAN.md) identifies the missing requirements.

Validation used an isolated local candidate copy and an isolated Python environment populated from already installed dependency distributions. It did not prove that a fresh network-free installation from publicly downloadable wheels is possible; a wheelhouse is not included. Observed versions are pinned. Platform and numerical-library changes may affect floating-point results or runtime.
